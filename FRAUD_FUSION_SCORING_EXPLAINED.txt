================================================================================
 SYNTHETICSHIELD — FRAUD FUSION SCORING: HOW A CLAIM GETS ITS SCORE
 (config version 0.1.0-design · thresholds and weights from scoring_config.yaml)
================================================================================

THE BIG PICTURE
---------------
Every claim is analyzed by SIX independent signals, each scored 0-100 where
HIGHER = MORE FRAUD-SUSPICIOUS:

  Level 1 — Metadata Analysis
    1. metadata     (weight 0.10)  EXIF/container/PDF rule checks, duplicate-
                                   evidence hashes, timestamp & GPS contradictions
  Level 2 — AI Manipulation Analysis
    2. image        (weight 0.22)  in-house pixel forensics (ELA, JPEG double-
                                   quantization, noise consistency, GAN frequency)
    3. video        (weight 0.22)  per-frame pixel battery + temporal checks
                                   (noise discontinuity, optical-flow jumps)
    4. audio        (weight 0.18)  voice deepfake detector (Resemble AI)
    5. text         (weight 0.08)  AI-written-text detectors (GPTZero + Pangram,
                                   cross-checked)
  Level 3 — Consistency Check
    6. consistency  (weight 0.20)  cross-evidence rules: narrative contradictions,
                                   weather records, reverse image search,
                                   per-part price verification

The engine is fully deterministic — no AI language model is involved in
computing the score. Same inputs always produce the same score.

STEP 1 — WEIGHTED BASE SCORE
----------------------------
  Base = 0.10·metadata + 0.22·image + 0.22·video + 0.18·audio
       + 0.08·text + 0.20·consistency

If a signal is unavailable (vendor outage) or not applicable (e.g. no video
submitted, statement too short), it is dropped and the remaining weights are
re-normalized to sum to 1.0 — a missing signal can never silently push a
claim toward approval.

STEP 2 — WORST-SIGNAL ESCALATION
--------------------------------
One near-certain fake must never be averaged away. If any LEVEL 2
AI-MANIPULATION signal (image, video, audio, text — only these can escalate)
reaches 90 or more:

  Final = max(Base, worst_signal − 5)

Exception by design: an external reverse-image-search hit (claim photo found
on the public web, rule C-H3) also escalates, as a virtual signal of at
least 92, even though it lives in the consistency check.

Metadata and consistency rule-point totals alone can NEVER trigger
escalation — they only contribute through their weights.

STEP 3 — ROUTING (the engine routes, it NEVER denies)
-----------------------------------------------------
  Final  <  15   → AUTO_APPROVE        (genuine — instant payout)
  15 ≤ Final ≤ 85 → HUMAN_REVIEW       (moderator decides)
  Final  >  85   → SIU_INVESTIGATION   (5-officer quorum vote)

Safety overrides (can only UPGRADE auto-approve to human review, never
downgrade): image/video analysis failed on media that was actually
submitted, or 3+ of the 6 signals unavailable.

Every score is stored append-only with its full decomposition — each
sub-score, each triggered rule, the weights used, and whether escalation
fired. Re-scoring creates a new version; history is never overwritten.

================================================================================
 CASE 1 — GENUINE CLAIM → AUTO_APPROVE
================================================================================
A real customer rear-ended at a light. Phone photos with intact camera EXIF,
a natural voice note, a hand-written description.

  Signal        Score   Why so low
  ---------     -----   ----------------------------------------------------
  metadata        6     intact camera EXIF consistent with claim (earned the
                        -8 credit against a minor +8 stripped-EXIF flag on
                        one WhatsApp-forwarded photo)
  image           4     normal JPEG compression, consistent sensor noise,
                        no double-quantization periodicity
  video           5     stable noise profile across frames, plausible motion
  audio           3     natural human voice, no synthesis markers
  text            4     human-written statement (detectors agree)
  consistency     7     voice, written statement and photos all describe
                        rear damage; claimed cost at market rate

  Base  = 0.10·6 + 0.22·4 + 0.22·5 + 0.18·3 + 0.08·4 + 0.20·7
        = 0.60 + 0.88 + 1.10 + 0.54 + 0.32 + 1.40  =  4.84
  Escalation: highest AI signal is 5 → far below 90 → does not fire.
  FINAL = 4.84  →  < 15  →  AUTO_APPROVE

What happens next: the valuation agent checks the claimed amount against a
live market estimate for the damaged part, caps the payout if inflated, the
payout is initiated automatically, and the customer is notified. Officers
still see the claim (read-only) with its full score division.

Worth noting: a photo with stripped EXIF alone (metadata = 8) contributes
only 0.10 × 8 = 0.8 points — deliberately weak, because WhatsApp/Instagram
strip EXIF benignly. It can never block an otherwise clean claim.

================================================================================
 CASE 2 — SUSPICIOUS-BUT-INCONCLUSIVE → HUMAN_REVIEW (moderator)
================================================================================
A claim where the photo was edited (maybe innocently — brightness, crop,
or maybe not), but nothing screams "fake".

  Signal        Score   Why medium
  ---------     -----   ----------------------------------------------------
  metadata       45     Photoshop software tag on a photo submitted as an
                        original capture (+35), modified 3h after capture
                        with an editor tag present (+20 would stack — here
                        total clamped from triggered rules ≈ 45)
  image          60     localized error-level hotspot + noise variance
                        inconsistency in one region — editing indicators,
                        but below the "near-certain" range
  video          15     clean
  audio          12     natural voice
  text           20     mostly human-written, mild anomalies
  consistency    25     claimed part cost ~2× market median (+15), one minor
                        discrepancy between voice and written statement (+10)

  Base  = 0.10·45 + 0.22·60 + 0.22·15 + 0.18·12 + 0.08·20 + 0.20·25
        = 4.50 + 13.20 + 3.30 + 2.16 + 1.60 + 5.00  =  29.76
  Escalation: highest AI signal is image at 60 → below 90 → does not fire.
  FINAL = 29.76  →  between 15 and 85  →  HUMAN_REVIEW

What happens next: the least-loaded moderator is assigned. The dashboard
shows the per-level score division (so the moderator sees it was the image
signal and the price inflation driving the score), the specific findings
("Photo IMG_2201.jpg carries an Adobe Photoshop software tag…"), and the
copilot can explain any number on demand. The moderator approves (payout)
or rejects (with reason) — the engine itself never denies.

================================================================================
 CASE 3 — AI-FABRICATED EVIDENCE → SIU_INVESTIGATION
================================================================================
Fraud attempt: AI-generated damage photos, cloned voice note, a story that
doesn't hold together.

  Signal        Score   Why high
  ---------     -----   ----------------------------------------------------
  metadata       85     no camera make/model on an "in-app capture" (+20),
                        editing trace (+35), capture timestamp after the
                        claim was submitted (+35) → clamped at 85 triggered
  image          96     strong JPEG double-quantization periodicity, flat
                        high-frequency spectrum typical of diffusion output,
                        noise inconsistency across regions
  video          20     (dashcam clip itself genuine)
  audio          30     some synthetic markers, inconclusive alone
  text           55     statement likely AI-polished (detectors disagree,
                        averaged, disagreement noted as a finding)
  consistency    88     voice says front collision, photos show rear damage
                        (+45, hard contradiction); claimed hail timing
                        contradicted by weather records (+45) → clamped 88

  Base  = 0.10·85 + 0.22·96 + 0.22·20 + 0.18·30 + 0.08·55 + 0.20·88
        = 8.50 + 21.12 + 4.40 + 5.40 + 4.40 + 17.60  =  61.42

  On averaging alone this would land in HUMAN_REVIEW — that is exactly the
  trap worst-signal escalation exists for:

  Escalation: image = 96 ≥ 90  →  Final = max(61.42, 96 − 5) = 91
  FINAL = 91  →  > 85  →  SIU_INVESTIGATION  (escalation_source = image)

What happens next: 5 least-loaded SIU officers are assigned. Confirming
fraud requires a UNANIMOUS quorum; a single "clear" vote clears the claim
and pays it out. The SIU panel sees the full division — including the fact
that one near-certain signal (image 96) drove the routing, not the average.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 VARIANT — THE STOLEN-PHOTO CASE (escalation from Level 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything looks mild (Base ≈ 25, consistency = 60) but the reverse image
search finds the "damage photo" on a used-car marketplace listing from last
year (rule C-H3, +50, escalation-eligible):

  Virtual signal = max(60, 92) = 92 ≥ 90
  Final = max(Base, 92 − 5) = 87  →  > 85  →  SIU_INVESTIGATION

A web-published photo is treated as near-certain misuse of evidence even
when every other signal is quiet.

================================================================================
 QUICK REFERENCE
================================================================================
  Weights        image .22 · video .22 · consistency .20 · audio .18 ·
                 metadata .10 · text .08   (sum = 1.0, re-normalized if
                 signals are missing)
  Escalation     any L2 signal ≥ 90 → Final = max(Base, signal − 5);
                 C-H3 web match acts as a 92 virtual signal
  Routing        < 15 auto-approve · 15–85 moderator · > 85 SIU
  Invariants     deterministic · decomposable · append-only · never denies
  Calibration    all numbers are design-stage values in scoring_config.yaml,
                 pending calibration on labeled historical claims
